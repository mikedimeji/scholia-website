Before you write

Two things make a problem much faster to fix, and you can skip them if you'd rather just tell us what happened:

  • The book and the page. Most problems are specific to one edition, and we can usually reproduce it with the title alone.
  • A screenshot. Especially if an answer looked wrong — what was on screen tells us far more than a description of it.

Things people ask

It put me in the wrong chapter. Scan a page with a printed chapter heading on it if you can, or set the chapter by hand from the book's menu. If it happens repeatedly on one book, tell us which — that's a bug in how we know that book, not something you're doing wrong.

It won't read my page. Flatten the spine, avoid a shadow across the gutter, and get the whole text block in frame. Very small print and heavily foxed pages are genuinely harder.

It spoiled something. Please tell us, with the book and roughly where you were. Spoiler protection is the promise the whole app rests on and we treat a leak as the most serious kind of bug.

I want my data deleted. Your books and conversations live on your phone — deleting the app removes them. If you made an account, you can delete it from Settings inside the app, or email us and we'll do it.

Billing

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel from Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
