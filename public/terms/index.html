Scholia is an iPhone app that answers questions about the physical book you are reading. You photograph a page, the text is read on your device, and we use that text to work out where you are in the book and answer what you ask.

It is a reading companion. It is not a substitute for reading, and it is not a source you should rely on for anything consequential.
